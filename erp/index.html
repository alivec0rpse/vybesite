How it works

One system, instead of nine spreadsheets.

Most campuses do not lack software. They have too much of it. An admissions register in one place, a fee ledger in another, attendance in a third and academics in a fourth, none of which agree with each other by the end of a term. Vybe ERP is the single record those four things should have been sharing all along.

A student record that runs from admission to alumni

A student is created once, at enquiry, and that record carries forward from there. Admission details, fee history, attendance, marks and staff remarks all hang off the same profile instead of being re-keyed into a new sheet each academic year. When the office needs to tell a parent what was paid, what was missed or what was scored, it is one lookup rather than four.

Fees, collected online and reconciled automatically

Fees are collected online with an instant receipt generated at the moment of payment, so there is no gap between the money arriving and the ledger showing it. Outstanding balances are visible to the office and to the parent at the same time, and the WhatsApp bot pushes reminders directly rather than relying on a note in a diary that never reaches home. Staff payroll logs sit in the same system, which means the institution is not running its outgoings on a separate spreadsheet.

Attendance without a register

The ERP integrates directly with the biometric scanners already installed on campus, so attendance is captured at the door and written straight to the student record. No class register, no end-of-month data entry, no discrepancy between what the teacher wrote and what the office totalled. Parents get attendance alerts on WhatsApp the same day, which is the point at which a pattern of absence is still worth acting on.

The academic work the office actually dreads

Timetables, exam seating plans and report cards are generated rather than assembled by hand. Those are the three jobs that reliably consume a week of somebody’s term. AI assistance goes a step further and helps staff draft exam papers, score sheets and study summaries, so teachers are reviewing and correcting instead of starting from a blank page.

Three portals, three different views of the same truth

Students, parents and staff each get their own login. Parents see academics, attendance and fee status without having to phone the office; students see their own timetable and results; staff see the administrative side. Because all three read from one record, nobody is ever working from a stale copy, and the front desk stops answering questions the system could answer by itself.

Where the data lives

Everything runs on Microsoft Azure. Data is encrypted, automated encrypted backups are taken daily, and we target 99.9% uptime. For an institution this matters most on the two or three days a year when the whole parent body logs in at once, like a fee deadline or a results announcement, and the system simply has to hold.

Customisable, because no two campuses are run the same way

Grading schemes, fee structures, attendance rules and academic calendars differ between a CBSE school, a state-board college and an autonomous institution, and an ERP that insists on one model forces the campus to change its process to suit the software. Vybe ERP is fully customisable, and where a requirement falls outside the product entirely, our development team builds it as part of the deployment rather than logging it as a feature request.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Vybe ERP?

add

Vybe ERP is a completely customisable, cloud-based Educational Resource Planning suite. It replaces fragmented administrative tasks (admissions, fees, attendance, academics) with one centralised, secure dashboard for schools and colleges.

Does Vybe ERP support biometric attendance?

add

Yes. The ERP integrates directly with campus biometric scanners so attendance is captured automatically, with no manual registers or spreadsheet reconciliation.

Can parents and students log in separately?

add

Yes. Students, parents and staff each get a dedicated portal login, so parents can track academics, attendance and fee status without going through the office.

How are fees collected?

add

Vybe ERP handles online fee collection with instant receipt generation, and keeps payroll logs for staff in the same system.

Is our data backed up and encrypted?

add

All data is encrypted, and the system takes automated encrypted backups daily to secure Microsoft Azure cloud servers. We target 99.9% server uptime.

What can the AI and WhatsApp features do?

add

The WhatsApp bot sends instant fee reminders and attendance alerts to parents. AI assistance helps staff generate exam papers, score sheets and study summaries.
