FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Vybe Technologies do?

add

Vybe Technologies is a software company based in Bengaluru. Our flagship products are Vybe ERP for school and college administration and Vybe CMS for 100% cashless cafeterias. We set up smart classrooms (interactive flat panels, online and hybrid class systems and writing boards) and handle workspace and email setup: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and Zoho Mail, staff and student accounts, single sign-on and secure online classes. Alongside those, Vybe IT Solutions takes on custom software work for businesses of any size or sector: web and mobile apps, business ERPs, retail POS, bespoke SaaS and internal tools, together with cybersecurity and managed IT. If your requirement does not match a product off our shelf, we build it to fit.

Who does Vybe work with?

add

The ERP and cafeteria products are built for schools, colleges and corporate campuses: student profiles, fees, payroll, biometric attendance and academics on one side, and a cafeteria system engineered to hold up during peak rush hours on the other. Our custom development, cybersecurity and cloud teams work well beyond education, with businesses in retail, hospitality, healthcare, gaming and professional services.

Can Vybe build software that is specific to our campus?

add

Yes. The ERP is fully customisable, and Vybe IT Solutions builds bespoke web and mobile applications (educational portals, business ERPs, retail POS and custom SaaS) to an institution’s exact operational requirements.

Do you set up smart classrooms?

add

Yes, smart classroom setup end to end: smart interactive flat panels sized to the room, online and hybrid class systems (classroom PC, camera, chat monitor, lighting and audio), UPS-backed power and cabling, and teacher training at handover. The panels are Logic, supplied through JS Global Industries in Bengaluru, the authorised distributor for the range, and a partner and dealer we work with for classroom boards as well.

Which vendor programmes is Vybe part of?

add

Four, and they are different kinds of thing. Ingram Micro: registered distribution partner, which is how we source and license the hardware and software we resell. AWS Partner Network: registered partner at the entry tier. Google Cloud Partner Advantage: programme member, covering Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace deployments. Microsoft for Startups: a grant of Azure credits awarded to us as a startup, not a partnership. None of the four is an accreditation, a certification, or any vendor’s endorsement of our work.
