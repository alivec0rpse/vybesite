FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Vybe CMS?

add

Vybe CMS is a comprehensive SaaS platform for institutional cafeterias. It eliminates campus food queues, automates vendor payouts and gives the institution real-time analytics across every stall.

How do students collect their food?

add

Students order from the app and are given an auto-generated QR code. The vendor scans it at the Service Terminal, which marks the order complete instantly, with no verbal confirmation and no queue.

How do vendors get paid?

add

Payouts are automated and powered by PhonePe. Funds are split at checkout, routing the food cost directly to the vendor’s bank account. Students can also pay from the zero-fee closed-loop Vybe Wallet.

Can students order ahead of a break?

add

Yes. Students can place an order from class and schedule it for ‘Break 1’ or ‘Break 2’, which lets kitchens prep intelligently instead of absorbing one large rush.

Do students need to install an app from the app store?

add

No. Vybe CMS is built as a PWA, so it installs to any iOS or Android home screen straight from the browser, with no app store approval required.

What does the kitchen see?

add

The Kitchen Display System (KDS) is a dedicated interface that separates immediate orders from scheduled break orders, with live order-prep tracking. Vendors can also mark items out of stock instantly or upload entire menus as JSON.

Is Vybe CMS being used anywhere today?

add

Yes. It is implemented at Christ Academy Institute for Advanced Studies (CAIAS).
