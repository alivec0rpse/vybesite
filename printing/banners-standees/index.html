In detail

Banners, standees & signage

Banners

Flex and vinyl, printed to whatever size you need rather than to a fixed list. Star flex for general outdoor use, backlit for a lit frame, blockout where light must not come through from behind. Finished with hemmed edges and eyelets as standard, and with rods or rope if you tell us how it is going up. Give us the finished size and the place it hangs, and we will flag it if the proportions are going to fight the space.

Standees and backdrops

Roll-up standees in the usual 6 by 3 feet, and wider where the stand allows, supplied with the stand and a carry bag. Backdrops and step-and-repeat walls for events, printed on fabric or flex with a frame if you need one. The thing worth remembering with a roll-up is that the bottom few inches sit inside the cassette, so anything important placed there disappears. We check for that before printing.

Signage and graphics

Foam board, sunboard and acrylic for indoor signage, name boards and directional signs. Vinyl for walls, windows and vehicles, including frosted film for glass partitions and one-way vision for windows you still want to see out of. Tell us the surface it is sticking to, because that decides which material is still there in six months.

Artwork for large format

Large format is more forgiving on resolution than paper is, because it gets read from a distance. It is not forgiving of a file that was already small. A logo pulled off a website, or a design forwarded through a chat app, has usually been compressed twice over, and it shows at banner size. Send the original file at whatever size it is and we will scale it. If it will not hold up, you hear that before it prints rather than after.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What resolution does my artwork need to be?

add

Send the original file rather than aim at a number. Large format is viewed from a distance, so it needs far less resolution than a brochure does, but that only helps if the file has not already been compressed. A screenshot or a forwarded copy has lost detail that cannot be put back. We open every file at print size before quoting and tell you plainly if it will not hold.

What size are roll-up standees?

add

The common one is 6 by 3 feet, supplied with the stand and a carry bag, and wider sizes are available where the stand allows. Keep anything important out of the bottom few inches of the design, because that strip rolls into the cassette and cannot be seen once it is set up.

Do you install banners and signage?

add

Delivery in and around Bengaluru is normal. For signage that needs mounting, tell us when you send the artwork and we will confirm whether installation can be arranged for your site and what it would involve, rather than assume it either way.

How long will an outdoor banner last?

add

It depends on the material and on the weather it takes. Star flex is the usual choice for a short outdoor campaign; vinyl on a rigid board lasts considerably longer. Tell us how long it has to stand and where, and we will recommend the material for that rather than the cheapest one on the list.
