Vybe audits, Vybe certificates. Read this first.

We read your premises, your records and your arrangements against the standard that applies to them, write down what we find, and where the audit passes we issue a certificate and a badge. Every audit here is a Vybe audit and every certificate is issued by Vybe Technologies.

Where an audit names a standard — FSSAI Schedule 4, the National Building Code, POCSO, the POSH Act, the DPDP Act, the UN SDGs — that names the yardstick we read you against. It is not a claim of approval, affiliation or recognition by whoever wrote it. We are not accredited by, affiliated to, appointed by or recognised by FSSAI, the Fire and Emergency Services, any Pollution Control Board, NAAC, NIRF, the Labour Department, the Data Protection Board or any other authority or certification body.

So our certificate is not a licence, an NOC, a hygiene rating, an accreditation or a finding of legal compliance, it carries no authority’s mark, and it must not be displayed or described as though it did. It is verifiable on our own portal and nowhere else. If somebody is asking your organisation for an official certificate, ours is not it, and we will tell you which body issues the one they want rather than sell you ours instead.

In detail

Kitchen & food business audits

Why a Vybe audit is worth anything

Because the official one is not a conversation. An FSSAI inspection, a customer’s audit or an aggregator’s hygiene check arrives, looks, and writes down what it finds, and by then the finding exists. A Vybe audit is the same walk with none of the consequences attached: the same cupboards opened, the same records asked for, the same questions put to the same staff, and a written answer you can act on while acting on it is still cheap. The value is entirely in the gap between the two, and nothing about our certificate is meant to close that gap on paper instead of in the kitchen.

What we actually do on site

We walk the whole line, from where deliveries land to where waste leaves, in service hours rather than after a deep clean, because a kitchen that has been scrubbed for a visitor tells you nothing. Fridges get opened and temperatures read. Records get asked for — supplier bills, water test reports, medical fitness certificates, the pest control file, the cleaning schedule — and the answer "it is with the accountant" is itself a finding. We talk to the people actually handling food, not only to the manager, because the gap between the written procedure and the practised one is most of what an audit is for.

What the report looks like

A written report with photographs, delivered within a few working days. Every finding names the Schedule 4 requirement it is read against, so it is arguable rather than a matter of opinion, and is graded: things that would fail an inspection or make somebody ill, things that would draw a comment, and things worth doing when you next have the kitchen closed. The fix list is ordered the same way. We would rather hand you six findings you will act on than sixty you will file.

Where our opinion stops

We read a kitchen against a published standard and we tell you what we see. We do not test food, we do not test water — we look at whether you have had it tested and by whom — and we do not certify anything to anybody else’s standard but our own. Where a finding needs a licensed professional, an electrical contractor, a food technologist, a structural engineer, we say so and we say to bring one in. The report and the certificate are documents from us, for you and for whoever you choose to show them to, and they carry no standing with any regulator.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this an FSSAI audit?

add

No. It is a Vybe audit conducted to the FSSAI Schedule 4 standard, which is a different sentence and we are careful to say it that way. Under the Food Safety and Standards (Food Safety Auditing) Regulations, 2018 a third-party food safety audit is carried out by an auditing agency recognised by FSSAI, and the result feeds back to the authority. We are not a recognised auditing agency and we are not affiliated to FSSAI in any way. What we do is read your kitchen against the same Schedule 4 requirements and tell you what would be found. It does not go to FSSAI, it does not change your inspection history, and it does not affect your hygiene rating.

Is the certificate an FSSAI certificate?

add

No, and it does not look like one. It is a Vybe Hygiene & Food Safety Certificate, issued by Vybe Technologies, carrying our name, our verification number and nobody else’s mark. It says your kitchen was audited by us to the Schedule 4 standard on a date and met our threshold. It is not an FSSAI licence, an FSSAI approval or an FSSAI hygiene rating, it must not be displayed or described as though it were, and your actual FSSAI licence is a separate thing we can also file for you.

Will the audit shut the kitchen for a day?

add

No. We walk a working kitchen during working hours, which is the whole point — a kitchen cleaned for a visitor is not the kitchen an inspector sees. A typical single-outlet audit is two to four hours on site, most of which is us staying out of the way. The records review usually takes longer than the walk.

What does it cost?

add

It is quoted rather than published, because a twelve-seat café and a central production kitchen feeding four outlets are not the same job. The things that move the number are floor area, how many kitchens and stores there are, whether the pest control and records review is included, and whether you want the re-check afterwards. Tell us what you run on WhatsApp and you will have a figure before anything is booked.

Do you fix what you find?

add

Some of it. Signage, records, schedules, the display material Schedule 4 asks for and the training register are things we can put right as part of the work. Anything that needs a contractor — a new exhaust, a floor regraded, a wall tiled — we describe precisely enough that you can get it quoted, and we do not take a cut from whoever quotes it. We also do not require you to use us for the fixes to get the re-check.

Can you audit a cloud kitchen or a home-based food business?

add

Yes, and the smaller ones are often where it matters most, because a cloud kitchen carries all the food safety risk of a restaurant with none of the staff to spread the work across. The standard is the same Schedule 4; what changes is how much of it applies. If you are also still unregistered, an FSSAI registration or licence is a separate piece of work and we will say which one you need.
