info

Vybe audits, Vybe certificates. Read this first.

We read your premises, your records and your arrangements against the standard that applies to them, write down what we find, and where the audit passes we issue a certificate and a badge. Every audit here is a Vybe audit and every certificate is issued by Vybe Technologies.

Where an audit names a standard, whether that is FSSAI Schedule 4, the National Building Code, POCSO, the POSH Act, the DPDP Act or the UN SDGs, that names the yardstick we read you against. It is not a claim of approval, affiliation or recognition by whoever wrote it. We are not accredited by, affiliated to, appointed by or recognised by FSSAI, the Fire and Emergency Services, any Pollution Control Board, NAAC, NIRF, the Labour Department, the Data Protection Board or any other authority or certification body.

So our certificate is not a licence, an NOC, a hygiene rating, an accreditation or a finding of legal compliance, it carries no authority’s mark, and it must not be displayed or described as though it did. It is verifiable on our own portal and nowhere else. If somebody is asking your organisation for an official certificate, ours is not it, and we will tell you which body issues the one they want rather than sell you ours instead.

  • balance

    POSH Act 2013 standard

    Vybe POSH Compliance Audit

    Internal Committee, policy, training and the annual report, mandatory at ten employees, with a penalty behind it.

    What it covers

    • check_circle Whether an Internal Committee exists at all, and whether one exists at every office with ten or more employees rather than only at head office
    • check_circle Its composition: a Presiding Officer who is a woman employed at a senior level, at least half the members women, and an external member from an NGO or with experience of issues relating to sexual harassment
    • check_circle Whether the external member is genuinely external and is being paid the fees the Rules provide for
    • check_circle The written policy: whether it exists, whether it says what the Act requires, and whether it has reached anybody
    • check_circle Display at a conspicuous place of the penal consequences and the Internal Committee’s constitution, which the Act requires and almost nobody does
    • check_circle The annual report to the District Officer, whether it has been filed, and for which years
    • check_circle Awareness programmes for employees and orientation for IC members, and the records of both
    • check_circle The complaint register, the procedure and the statutory timelines, and whether a complaint could be handled without improvising
    • check_circle Coverage of interns, trainees, contract staff, probationers and people working from home, all of whom count

    Read against

    The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the Rules made under it. The obligation to constitute an Internal Committee bites at ten or more employees.

    If it passes

    Vybe POSH Compliance Certificate and badge, issued by Vybe Technologies, valid one year.

    Failure to constitute an Internal Committee or to comply with the Act carries a fine of up to ₹50,000 for a first contravention. On a repeat, the penalty is doubled and the licence or registration under which the business operates can be cancelled or not renewed.

    What it is notWe review your arrangements and report on them. FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are these official audits?

add

No, and that is the first thing we say rather than the last. Every audit here is a Vybe audit and none of them is a statutory or regulatory audit. We are not the fire department, not an FSSAI-recognised auditing agency under the Food Safety Auditing Regulations, 2018, not a Labour Department inspector, not an appointed Data Auditor under the DPDP Act, and not connected to NAAC or NIRF. When an audit names a standard, that names what we read you against. It is not a claim that whoever wrote the standard has approved us or you.

Then what is the point of an unofficial audit?

add

That the official one is not a conversation. When a fire inspection, a food safety audit, a vendor’s licence review or a POSH complaint arrives, it looks, it finds, and the finding exists from that moment. A Vybe audit is the same walk with none of the consequences attached, early enough that fixing things is a line item rather than a crisis. The entire value sits in the gap between our visit and the one that counts.

You issue certificates. What are they worth?

add

They are worth exactly what they say, which is a real thing and a limited one. A Vybe certificate says that we audited a named site against a named standard on a named date and that it met the threshold we publish for that audit. It carries our name, a verification number checkable on our own portal, and an expiry a year out. It is not a licence, an NOC, a hygiene rating, an accreditation or a finding of legal compliance, it carries no authority’s mark, and it must not be displayed or described as though it does. Plenty of parents, customers, tender committees and franchise partners are satisfied by a credible third party having looked and written it down. Anybody who needs an official certificate needs a different document, and we will tell you which body issues it.

Are you affiliated to FSSAI, NAAC, the fire department or any certifying body?

add

No, to all of them and to any others you can name. We are not accredited by, affiliated to, appointed by, empanelled with or recognised by FSSAI, any fire authority, any Pollution Control Board, NAAC, NIRF, the UGC, the Labour Department, the Data Protection Board, the BSA, any software vendor, NABCB or any member of the International Accreditation Forum. We do not claim that our certificates are recognised by any of them, and if you ever see anybody suggesting otherwise on our behalf, tell us and we will correct it.

Does everybody pass?

add

No, and a certificate nobody can fail is worth nothing to the people you would show it to. Where an audit closes below the threshold we say so in writing, we tell you exactly what stands between you and it, and the re-check is there for when you have closed those items. We would rather issue you a certificate in three months that means something than one today that does not.

What do we actually get at the end?

add

A written report per audit, delivered within a few working days of the visit, with photographs of what we found. Each finding cited to the clause, rule or Goal it is read against, so you can argue with it on the merits instead of taking our word. Findings graded into what would fail an inspection or hurt somebody, what would draw a comment, and what is worth doing when you next have the chance. A fix list in that order. And where the audit passes, the Vybe certificate and digital badge described above.

What does an audit cost?

add

It is scoped first and then quoted, and we do not publish a figure because there is no honest one to publish. A single POSH review for one office and a six-audit engagement across a campus with four blocks and fourteen buses are not the same work. Floor area, number of sites, fleet size, headcount, how many audits you want and whether you want the re-check are what move it. Tell us what you run on WhatsApp or at legal@vybe.net.in and you will have a scope and a figure before anything is booked.

Do you fix what you find?

add

The paperwork, yes: policies, registers, schedules, display notices, the documentation an application or an inspection asks for, and the training records behind them. That can be included in the scope. Anything needing a contractor (an exhaust replaced, a floor regraded, a distribution board rewired, a camera moved) we describe precisely enough for you to get it quoted, and we take nothing from whoever quotes it. You are not required to use us for the fixes to get the re-check or the certificate.

Who carries out the audit?

add

Our own team, working to a written checklist per audit so that two visits to the same place produce comparable reports. Where a finding needs a licensed professional to resolve or to sign off, whether that is a chartered electrical safety inspector, a structural engineer, a food technologist, a lawyer or an external Internal Committee member, we say so in the report and we say to bring one in. We would rather mark the boundary of our competence than blur it.

Is what you find confidential?

add

Yes. The report is yours. Nothing goes to any regulator, vendor, industry body or third party from us, and nothing is used as a reference without your written agreement. The one exception is the certificate, which exists to be shown and is checkable by anybody you give the number to. The one thing we will not do is stay quiet about an immediate danger to a person, or about something that looks like it triggers a mandatory reporting obligation, a POCSO disclosure in particular. In that situation you will hear it from us on the day, in plain words, along with what the law expects of you.

Do you audit outside Bengaluru?

add

Bengaluru and the surrounding districts are the normal working area and there is no travel charge inside it. Further afield is possible, with travel quoted separately and openly in the scope. For the document-led audits (POSH, software licensing, DPDP and labour law) a good part of the work is records, systems and conversations, so distance matters less. The site-based ones cannot be done remotely and we will not pretend they can.

We already had an audit. Why another one?

add

If it was recent, thorough and you have worked through it, you may not need one. Send us the report and we will tell you plainly whether a second opinion is worth your money. Sometimes the answer is that a re-check against the existing list is all you need, and that is a much smaller piece of work. What is usually worth revisiting is anything older than a year, anything from before a building, a block, a kitchen or a fleet changed, and anything covering only one of the areas you are now being asked about.
