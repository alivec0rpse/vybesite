    POSH Act 2013 standard

    Vybe POSH Compliance Audit

    Internal Committee, policy, training and the annual report, mandatory at ten employees, with a penalty behind it.

    What it covers

    • check_circle Whether an Internal Committee exists at all, and whether one exists at every office with ten or more employees rather than only at head office
    • check_circle Its composition: a Presiding Officer who is a woman employed at a senior level, at least half the members women, and an external member from an NGO or with experience of issues relating to sexual harassment
    • check_circle Whether the external member is genuinely external and is being paid the fees the Rules provide for
    • check_circle The written policy: whether it exists, whether it says what the Act requires, and whether it has reached anybody
    • check_circle Display at a conspicuous place of the penal consequences and the Internal Committee’s constitution, which the Act requires and almost nobody does
    • check_circle The annual report to the District Officer, whether it has been filed, and for which years
    • check_circle Awareness programmes for employees and orientation for IC members, and the records of both
    • check_circle The complaint register, the procedure and the statutory timelines, and whether a complaint could be handled without improvising
    • check_circle Coverage of interns, trainees, contract staff, probationers and people working from home, all of whom count

    Read against

    The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the Rules made under it. The obligation to constitute an Internal Committee bites at ten or more employees.

    If it passes

    Vybe POSH Compliance Certificate and badge, issued by Vybe Technologies, valid one year.

    Failure to constitute an Internal Committee or to comply with the Act carries a fine of up to ₹50,000 for a first contravention. On a repeat, the penalty is doubled and the licence or registration under which the business operates can be cancelled or not renewed.

    What it is notWe review your arrangements and report on them. We do not sit on your Internal Committee, we do not conduct inquiries into complaints, and neither the audit nor the certificate files your annual report or discharges any obligation under the Act. A Vybe certificate is not a finding of compliance by any authority and would not answer a District Officer. Where you need an external IC member or a lawyer, we will say so.

In detail

Office & workplace compliance audits

POSH is the one with a deadline already passed

Every other audit here is prudent. POSH is overdue. The obligation to constitute an Internal Committee applies to every employer with ten or more employees, it applied from 2013, and it applies at each office separately rather than once for the company. The penalty for not having one is up to ₹50,000, and for a repeat it doubles and brings the possibility of the licence or registration the business trades under being cancelled. The part that catches people is not the committee, though. It is the rest of it: the external member who has to come from outside, the display of the committee’s constitution at a conspicuous place, the annual report to the District Officer, and the awareness sessions. We have yet to walk into an office that had all five and could show the records.

The software audit usually pays for itself

Not because of the exposure, although a letter from a vendor or from an industry body is an unpleasant way to spend a quarter, but because of what the inventory turns up in the other direction. Almost every office of any size is paying for seats nobody uses, for a subscription renewed by standing instruction for a product replaced two years ago, and for licences tied to laptops that left the building. The count runs in both directions and the second direction is usually the larger number. What we will not do is tell you the exposure is bigger than it is in order to sell you something: where the answer is that you are fine, the report says you are fine.

DPDP is a gap assessment, not a certificate of compliance

Nobody can make you DPDP compliant in a week, and anybody offering to should be shown the door. What is achievable is knowing exactly where you stand: what personal data you hold, whose it is, what you told them when you took it, who you have since given it to, how long you keep it, and what would happen if somebody asked for it back or if it leaked. Most organisations cannot answer more than two of those. The audit produces the answers and a prioritised list, and the first items on that list are usually unglamorous: a retention rule, a vendor contract clause, a grievance address that reaches a person. The certificate at the end says we audited you against the Act on a date. It does not say you are compliant, because that is not ours to say.

What you get, and what our opinion is worth

A written report per audit with a graded finding list, the provision each finding is read against, and a fix list ordered by exposure rather than by ease. Where the fix is a form, a register, a notice or a policy, we can do it as part of the work. Where it needs a lawyer, a chartered accountant, a licensed electrical contractor or an external IC member, we say so and we say it early. None of this is legal advice, none of it is a statutory audit, and the certificate is ours rather than any authority’s. It is a competent outside reading of your position, in writing, before somebody with the power to fine you forms their own.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

We have fewer than ten employees. Does POSH apply to us?

add

The obligation to constitute an Internal Committee starts at ten. Below that you still owe your employees a workplace free of sexual harassment, complaints go to the Local Committee constituted by the District Officer rather than to an internal one, and it is worth having a policy and knowing where to send somebody. If you are near the threshold, the sensible time to set the committee up is before you cross it, not after somebody counts.

Does this audit make us POSH compliant?

add

No. It tells you precisely where you are not, and it can produce the missing pieces: a policy that says what the Act requires, the display notice, the committee constitution, the annual report format, the awareness session. What it cannot do is serve on your Internal Committee, conduct an inquiry, or file anything on your behalf as though we were you. The external member of your IC has to come from outside the organisation and must not be us reviewing our own arrangement. The certificate records that we audited you against the Act; it is not a finding of compliance by any authority.

Is the software licence audit reported to anyone?

add

No. Nothing leaves your organisation. We are not the BSA, not a vendor’s SAM programme and not an OEM auditor, we are not affiliated to any software vendor, and we have no obligation or incentive to tell anybody what we found. That is the entire point of counting it yourself first.

Is the DPDP audit legal advice?

add

No. It is a technical and procedural gap assessment against the Act and Rules, written by people who read them carefully, and it will tell you where your practice and the text do not line up. Where the answer turns on a question of law, whether that is a legitimate use applying to you, how a contract should be drafted or whether you are a Significant Data Fiduciary, we will tell you that it is a lawyer’s question rather than answer it ourselves.

Can you do these remotely?

add

Partly. The POSH, software licence, DPDP and labour law audits are mostly documents, systems and conversations, and a good deal of that can be done remotely with a shorter visit at the end. The fire and workplace safety audit cannot be done remotely at all and we will not pretend otherwise. Outside Bengaluru, the document-led audits travel fine; for the site-based ones, ask and we will tell you honestly whether the travel is worth it to you.

What does a workplace audit cost?

add

Scoped first, then quoted. Headcount, number of offices, how many of the five audits you want and how much of it can be done remotely are what move the number. A single POSH review for one office is a small piece of work; a five-audit engagement across three locations is not. Tell us the shape of it at legal@vybe.net.in and a scope and figure come back before anything starts.
