Due to the fast-paced nature of campus cafeterias, cancellation policies are strictly enforced by the system:

  • Immediate Orders: Can only be cancelled within 5 minutes of placing the order, provided the vendor has not already marked it as "Preparing" or "Ready."
  • Scheduled Break 1 Orders: Must be cancelled before 10:00 AM on the day of pickup.
  • Scheduled Break 2 Orders: Must be cancelled before 12:30 PM on the day of pickup.
  • Once an order surpasses its cancellation window or is marked "Completed", it cannot be refunded.

Vybe provides two primary methods of payment: the closed-loop Vybe Wallet and direct Online Payment via Cashfree.

Wallet balances are tied to your campus ID and cannot be cashed out or transferred to external bank accounts. Unused wallet balances upon graduation or departure from the institution must be claimed as physical vendor credit within 30 days.
