4. Vybe CMS: cancellations and cut-offs

Cafeterias run to a clock, so cancellation windows are enforced by the system:

  • Immediate orders can be cancelled within 5 minutes of being placed, provided the vendor has not already marked the order "Preparing" or "Ready".
  • Scheduled Break 1 orders must be cancelled before 10:00 AM on the day of pickup.
  • Scheduled Break 2 orders must be cancelled before 12:30 PM on the day of pickup.
  • Once an order passes its cancellation window or is marked "Completed", it cannot be refunded.
