FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who should we contact — sales, support or legal?

add

Sales, on sales@vybe.net.in, for anything you do not have yet: a quote, a demo, a new campus, extra modules or hardware. Support, on support@vybe.net.in, for something already running that is not behaving. Legal, on legal@vybe.net.in, for contracts, data protection and privacy requests, security disclosures and invoicing disputes. If you pick the wrong one we will pass it across rather than send you back.

What if my enquiry is not resolved?

add

Start with sales, support or legal, whichever fits — they own the work and settle almost everything directly. If the matter is still unresolved after that, if you are not satisfied with how it was handled, or in a serious matter, you may bring it to our Grievance Officer at manjunath@vybe.net.in, who also receives privacy and data protection grievances. Please attach the original correspondence and the date it was sent.

What should we include in a first enquiry?

add

The more of this you can tell us up front, the more useful the first reply is: roughly how many students or staff the system has to cover, which of the products you are interested in, anything you are running today that the new system has to work alongside or replace, and the timeline you are working to. None of it is required to start a conversation.

Where are you located, and can we visit?

add

Our registered office is at #103, First Floor, 10th Main, Gaurav Nagar, J.P Nagar 7th Phase, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560078. You are welcome to come and see the products running — call ahead on +91 99641 77877 so somebody who can answer your questions is in the office.

Do you work with institutions outside Bengaluru?

add

Yes. We are based in Bengaluru and serve customers across India. The software is cloud-hosted, so an ERP or cafeteria deployment does not depend on how far away you are; on-site work such as smart classroom installation and hardware is scheduled per project.
